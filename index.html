What's actually animating

🦴

Skeletal rig

Every character has a bone hierarchy — hips, torso, neck, head, shoulders, elbows, wrists. Poses are computed per frame.

🚶

Walk-on motion

Characters walk into frame with alternating legs, arm counter-swing and vertical hip bob, then settle into an idle stance.

👄

Lip sync

Mouth opens and closes on a speech envelope that tracks the dialogue of the current beat, matched to the speaking character.

🎞️

Scene cutting

The storyline cuts between beats — wide establishing, medium dialogue, close-up reaction, action, climax, resolution.
